Echo Archive painted sprites (heroes/, creatures/).

Original EnglishGo artwork, generated on 2026-11-19 with ElevenLabs image generation
(model: bytedance-seedream-5.5, transparent background; three images had their
backgrounds removed with birefnet-v2-bg-removal), then trimmed and resized to
512x512 WebP so each figure stands on the bottom edge of its square.

Shared style prompt: hand-painted dark-fantasy storybook game illustration,
gouache and ink brushwork with visible paper texture, rim light, jewel-tone
palette per act (Act I midnight blue and violet, Act II brass and copper,
Act III deep purple and silver, Act IV parchment and void), full-body sprite,
heroes facing right and creatures facing left, suitable for a children's card game.

Each creature keeps the design of its earlier hand-drawn SVG, which remains in
src/features/echo-archive/components/Creatures.jsx as the fallback.

Card illustrations (cards/): one painting for each of the 47 Rare and Mythic
cards, generated on 2026-11-19 with the same model (bytedance-seedream-5.5,
16:9), then centre-cropped to the card's art window and saved as 560x272 WebP.
Shared style prompt: hand-painted storybook fantasy card illustration in
gouache and watercolor with ink linework and visible paper texture, soft rim
light, set in a magical ancient library of living ink and paper, palette by
card type (Attack crimson, ember and gold; Skill teal, sea blue and silver;
Power violet, indigo and gold), Mythic cards with an extra radiant gold glow,
no text. The card's glyph stays as the fallback while a painting loads.

Event scenes (events/): one painting for the Lantern Keeper and each of the 25
events, generated on 2026-11-20 with the same model (16:9) and saved as
960x540 WebP. Shared style prompt: hand-painted storybook fantasy scene
illustration in gouache and watercolor with ink linework and visible paper
texture, soft rim light, cozy yet mysterious mood, inside a magical ancient
library tower of living ink and paper, palette chosen per scene, no text.
The event's glyph is the fallback if a scene cannot load.
