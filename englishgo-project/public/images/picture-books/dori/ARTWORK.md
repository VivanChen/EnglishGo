# Dori and the Echo in the Mist — artwork

Built-in image_gen, 2026-09-09. Six original book-specific assets copied into this folder. Alpha transparency checked for the five paper cutouts; valley is opaque scenery. No raster postprocessing.

- `dori-alert.png`: Young moss-green triceratops, ochre freckles, ivory horns, amber eyes, alert right-facing full-body pose; refined natural-history watercolor and colored pencil, ivory paper edge, genuine transparency; no scenery, text or CGI.
- `dori-listening.png`: Reference dori-alert, same identity/colors; concerned listening pose turned left, feet planted, no crying; preserve medium and transparent background.
- `mother.png`: Reference dori-alert family style; mature sturdy adult triceratops, larger frill and longer horns, head lowered gently, left-facing; whole silhouette on transparent background.
- `valley.png`: Wide prehistoric fern valley with mist, fallen branch, distant cliffs, trail and rainwater channel; detailed watercolor/pencil, open center for characters, no animals or text.
- `fern.png`: Single giant tree fern with arching canopy, fibrous trunk and fiddleheads; botanical watercolor/pencil, moss/sage palette, transparent background.
- `footprints.png`: Isolated damp mud patch with three narrow pointed three-toed impressions, not raised feet; watercolor/pencil, moss edge and genuine transparency.

Shared rock/cloud paper props are reused as scenery; all dinosaur characters and the valley are new. This is an original fictional story, not a reconstruction of dinosaur behavior.
